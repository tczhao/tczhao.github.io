One shelf per year, ranked left to right. The first cover is the pick and carries the note; the other four are the rest of the shortlist, in order. Every cover links out to AniList.

What ranks
Broad critical and fan consensus, not personal taste. Where a year had an obvious landmark it takes first place even if something else is more fun to watch.
How years are set
First release in Japan. A series is filed under its premiere year, so long runs appear once. Split-cour sequels are listed separately only when they are usually judged as their own work.
What counts
TV series, theatrical films and OVAs compete in the same list. The 1980s need it: 1988 is carried by two films, not television.
Colour
Each decade takes its accent from how the work was actually made, from cel and airbrush through to digital compositing.
