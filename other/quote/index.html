    What this is

    A commonplace book, which is the old name for exactly this: a place you copy the lines worth keeping so you meet them again. Everything here is transcribed from my own notes rather than sourced, so treat it as what I concluded, not as what is true.

    Two entries are marked unwritten. They were headings I never filled in, and they stay visible so they keep asking.

    How the card picks

    The card at the top is chosen from the date, so it holds for the whole day and changes at local midnight. Pressing Another steps forward without committing.

    Unwritten entries never surface as a card. Everything else is equally likely, including the four-step plays, which are longer than a card wants to be but are the ones worth rehearsing.
