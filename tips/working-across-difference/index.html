Tip 02 · working with people

Impedance Match

Six dials people vary on, what to look for, and the same message sent to each end of each one. There is no wording that works on everybody, which is the point - what transfers between people is the reading, not the script.

None of these dials is a nationality, a generation or a personality type. Those give you a weak prior at best. The person in front of you gives you a better one inside two exchanges, and when the two disagree, the person wins.

/

The limit

Adapting the delivery is not adapting the message. If the plan is wrong you still say the plan is wrong - you are choosing the packaging, not the content. The moment the accommodation starts eating the substance, and the concern gets softened until it is no longer a concern, you have stopped working with someone and started failing them quietly.

The cost here is also not shared evenly. The person doing the adapting is usually the one with less power in the room, and "communicate better" is advice that gets handed down far more often than it gets handed up. If you are the senior person, most of the value on this page is in noticing which dials your team has silently been matching to you, and moving toward them for once.
