Tip 05 · getting promoted

Burden of Proof

Ten conversations on the way from running a team to running the people who run teams, each carried through to the reply you will get and what you say to it. Director is decided as a case, the standard is that you are already doing the job, and the case is heard by people who have not watched you work.

That last part is what makes it unlike every promotion before it. Up to here, doing the work well and being seen to was enough. From here the burden is on you to make it legible in a room you are not in, to people whose main question is what it would be like to have you at the table. Practicum has the daily practice for the level itself. This is the year before it.

Standing and limit

Same standing as the rest of this section: craft, not evidence. There is a literature on promotion and it mostly studies who gets promoted rather than what to say, so nothing above carries a number I would be willing to put next to a sentence. Treat it the way the section treats everything: worth doing, not worth citing.

The standard - already doing the job - is also not applied evenly. Who gets handed the ownerless problem, whose escalation reads as judgement and whose reads as pushiness, whose four sentences a sponsor repeats without being asked: none of that is about your delivery, and being told to just take scope by someone for whom it was cheap is its own kind of unhelpful. What is portable is the same as in Ask Price: the target named as a date, the case written in their units, the three answers decided before the room. That part survives a room that is not on your side. So does the pay conversation that goes with a yes, which Ask Price has and this page does not repeat.

None of it works if what you want is the title rather than the job. Director is further from the work, most of the week is spent in rooms deciding things you will never touch, and the people you are responsible for are ones you did not hire and will rarely see. For a fair share of excellent managers the right answer is not to go, and nothing on this page can tell you which share you are in. It is worth spending part of the year finding out, because the conversations above are hard to unsay.
