Tip 01 · working with people

Right of Way

Twelve plays for moving work through people who do not report to you, each with the sentence to actually send.

They work by making the ask cheap and its state visible, not by being clever. The manipulative version of any one of them burns the relationship the next one depends on.

/

The limit

None of this works on someone who is simply overloaded. A person with four things due this week and no way to drop any of them does not need a better-worded ask, and pressing one on them converts a capacity problem into a personal one. The fix there is fewer asks or a different owner, and it is a conversation with whoever sets their queue.

The plays also assume you are right that the work matters. Half the times I have wanted to push harder, the honest answer was that my thing was less important than theirs and I did not want it to be.
