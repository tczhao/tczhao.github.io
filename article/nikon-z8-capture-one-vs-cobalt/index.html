ColorChecker targets are X-Rite's published D50 values for ColorChecker Classic charts made after November 2014. The checker comparison finds the camera values that the left-hand profile renders as those colours, then shows the other profile's rendering of the same values. Colours are drawn with CSS lab(), so your browser maps them to your display.
