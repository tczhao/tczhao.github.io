18.06 Linear Algebra

All 35 lectures, one idea each: the result the lecture is actually carrying, the formula worth keeping, and the thing that catches people the first time.

The argument runs elimination → the four subspaces → orthogonality → eigenvalues → the SVD. On the way back down, almost every question is answered by the rank.

Lecture numbering and titles follow the OCW 18.06 Spring 2010 video series. The notes are mine; the course is Strang’s.
